# Employee notice template (synthetic)

A template for the fictional company Larkspur Systems (`larkspur.example`). It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by counsel; a real deployment adapts it with counsel for each jurisdiction (see [regulatory-notes.md](regulatory-notes.md)).

## Notice

**How Larkspur uses StaySignal for workforce planning**

**What it does.** People Analytics uses StaySignal to estimate how many people may leave a team or an organization over the next one, three or six months, so that People Partners can plan hiring, backfills and support for groups. It produces numbers about groups of people, never about you.

**What it does not do.** It does not produce a score, ranking or prediction about any individual that anyone can see, and it is never used to make or support a decision about an individual employee: not about pay, promotion, performance, discipline or employment. Individual estimates exist only briefly in memory while a group total is computed and are never stored.

**What data it uses.** Records Larkspur already holds for managing employment: your job, level, team, manager, location, employment type, hire, promotion and transfer dates, and base pay compared with the pay band for your role. It does not use performance ratings, health or other sensitive information, monitoring data, or free-text personnel files. Your location is used only to report results by site, never to estimate departures.

**Who sees the results.** People Analytics and People Partners see results for groups of at least 10 people (at least 50 for comparisons over time); People Partners see only the organizations they support. Results are rounded so that no individual can be singled out.

**How long data is kept.** Group results are kept for three years. The underlying employment records are kept for as long as Larkspur's records policy requires. Individual estimates are never kept.

**Your rights and questions.** You can ask People Analytics how StaySignal works, request a copy of the personal information Larkspur holds about you, and raise concerns with [contact]. Where local law gives you further rights, Larkspur will honor them.
